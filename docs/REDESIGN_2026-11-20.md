# Redesign and product photographs (20 Nov 2026, evening)

**The request.** The owner sent a 7-Eleven Bangladesh advert showing the green Mr. Noodles rice-stick pack, with these words: "you dont have to use stock photos from jsut the old website … look on the internet to find picutres of their product and replace any subpar images with them. In addition the websitd still looks very much AI slob, please use the design skills installed to elevate the UI/UX design".

**State:** built and checked on local branch `o2/redesign-2026-11-20`. **Not published.** The public preview is still `gh-pages` `5077ab8`. Publishing is the owner's word: `npm run pages:deploy`, then `pages:smoke --url` and `audit:metrics --url`. The way back from a publish is `gh-pages` `5077ab8`.

## Kept, because the owner chose them

These were kept on the owner's own instructions of 19–20 Nov:
- The rice palette: white, cream, natural gold and roasted brown.
- A drifting gradient ground.
- The liquid-glass top bar.
- The opening animation.
- The recoloured PK mark.

The design skills ban several of these. Gradients and glass are among the commonest "generated page" tells. The owner's words rank first, so they stay, made quieter:
- One daylight wash instead of four light blobs.
- No resting shadow on the bar.

Say the word and either goes.

## Changed

The direction is taste-skill's "the object, large" for the opening and "specimens" for the formats, inside the owner's palette. apple-design governs the motion and the materials.

| Before | Now |
|---|---|
| Small tracked capital labels over every section ("FORMATS", "OEM / PRIVATE LABEL") | Gone. The headings say what each section is. Labels that carry a tag (the 2014 words, Sample) stay, because the tag is a statement about the content. |
| Headline 68 px, sections in one even rhythm | One large scale in Trirong: the opening at 88 px, section titles to 70 px, tight tracking. Neighbouring sections each have their own composition. |
| Rounded cards with drop shadows, pill clusters ("Nature 100%" chips, dish names, jump links) | Flat surfaces, 6 px photo corners, no resting shadow. Lists set as a line of type with gold middots. Fully round buttons with one flat fill. |
| Process numbered 01–05 between hairlines | No numbers. One gold strand runs through the five steps, and their sentences start on one line (subgrid). |
| Muddy fades between the cream and the brown chapter | Clean edges. The brown chapter and the footer are flat roast with one soft gold light. |
| The 2014 blue pack, upscaled, in a rounded card | The current green 454 g pack, large, set on a warm field that runs up under the glass bar and is cropped by its edge. The caption sits under it. |
| Four 186 px close-ups from 2014, upscaled 3.4× | Vermicelli and rice stick are sharp open-licence close-ups, credited on the card. Flake and brown rice keep the 2014 files, because nothing honest and better exists. Three of the four "cooked" views are now the high-resolution serving photographs. |
| The footer as a sitemap | The name returns at 84 px as the close, and the columns sit beside it. |

**Not used:**
- The owner's own reference, the 7-Eleven advert. It prints a price in taka and has arrows drawn on it, and it looks generated.
- A shop photo with a Gemini mark.
- Lifestyle shots with shop logos.
- Every blue vermicelli pack. No usable photo of it exists online, so vermicelli shows a credited close-up, and the 2014 blue pack stays as the opening's fallback.

**What the current pack shows that the site did not know.** These are read from the photographs:
- The current pack prints "SAHA PAN KAO" with an SPK oval.
- It carries GMP, HACCP and halal marks.
- It weighs 454 g / 1 lb.
- The back label reads "WHITE RICE 90%, Tapioca starch 10%", manufacturing licence 12-2-02151, "dried by means of baking instead of exposing to the sun".

What changed on the site because of this:
- The rice-stick and Mr. Noodles sample ingredients now follow that label (still tagged Sample).
- No certificate, mark or "100% rice" claim was added.
- Separately, the 200 g lines sell as "ตรา ปั้นข้าว PANKAO" (pumpkin vermicelli; a yanang-leaf rice stick). They are evidence for the pumpkin and yanang slots, which stay hidden until the company confirms them.
