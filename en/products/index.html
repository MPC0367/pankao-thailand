Our rice-noodle formats

Dried rice noodles in different widths and shapes, made under our own name and for other brands. Specifications such as ingredients, pack weights and shelf life appear once they are confirmed against current labels.

Thai, English or Vietnamese names work.

Showing 5 formats

Rice vermicelli

dried rice vermicelli · rice noodles (fine)

Fine, round, white rice noodles, close in size to angel-hair pasta. Fine in texture and at their best in broth.

Illustrative photoPhoto: Popo le Chien / Wikimedia Commons, CC BY-SA 3.0 (opens in a new tab)

Rice stick

Chanthaburi rice noodles · Pad Thai noodles · flat rice noodles

Flat rice noodles, wider than vermicelli and cut to length. Chewier than finer rice noodles once blanched, and good with or without broth.

Illustrative photoPhoto: Horakvlado / Wikimedia Commons, CC BY-SA 4.0 (opens in a new tab)

Rice flake (Guay Jub)

Guay Jub sheets · rice flake noodles

Square or triangular sheets that curl into rolls in boiling water.

Brown-rice noodles

brown rice noodles

Rice noodles made from unpolished brown rice.

Mr. Noodles

Mr. Noodles rice noodles

Our own brand of dried rice vermicelli and dried rice stick, in clear bags: blue stripes for vermicelli, green for rice stick.
